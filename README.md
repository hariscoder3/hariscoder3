## 🧭 How I Work

**Discover → Prototype → Validate → Ship**

1. **Discover:** Get on the call with the client and dig past the request to the real problem.
2. **Prototype:** Put something clickable in front of people in days, not weeks.
3. **Validate:** Measure it instead of guessing, with manual tests plus evaluation suites that score query resolution.
4. **Ship:** Take it to production safely, cutting over only once the new setup is proven stable.

- **SellAgent AI — Real-Time Voice Agent** *(production, founding team)*

  `LiveKit · Deepgram · Cartesia · OpenAI · Anthropic Claude · RAG · AWS`

- **[ContractIQ — AI Legal Document Assistant](https://github.com/hariscoder3/ContractIQ)** *(built solo in 48 hours, Trae AI Zero Limits Hackathon)*
  Indexes contract text into a Zilliz vector database and retrieves the relevant clauses as grounding for each answer, cutting down on unsupported model responses.
  `Python · LangChain · Zilliz · Embeddings · Semantic Search`

- **AI Meeting Assistant** *(client project)*
  Transcribes advisor–client conversations and pulls out structured insights, summaries, and follow-up tasks.
  `Python · Flask · OpenAI · LangChain · Deepgram · AWS`

- **[HealthHive — Medical RAG Chatbot](https://huggingface.co/spaces/harris1/HealthHiveChatbot)** *(live demo)*
  Medical assistant using RAG over PubMed and Google Search to give cited answers, with session-only chat history.
  `Python · RAG · Llama · Together AI · Gradio`

- **[Uni-Trade — University Marketplace App](https://github.com/hariscoder3/Uni_Trade/tree/last)** *(final year project)*
  Student marketplace with .edu.pk verification, real-time bidding, ML-based recommendations, and NLP moderation of chat messages.
